How to use this tool

1. Pick the week

Use the ‹ › arrows or the date box at the top to choose the week. Each week is saved on its own. A brand-new week starts empty — you can press Copy last week to carry the previous week forward as a starting point.

2. Fill the roster

On the Weekly Roster tab, click any cell. A list of doctors appears, sorted best-fit first:

  • Recommended — fits the site, no clashes.
  • Possible — works, but check the note.
  • Has conflicts — would double-book the doctor or break a hard rule.

If the doctor you want is not on the list, press + Add a new doctor at the bottom of the list (it also appears when a search finds nobody). Fill in the short form — the status, pool and OHD boxes are already set to suit that slot — then press Add & assign to add them and put them straight into the cell, or Add only to add them and carry on. Either way the doctor is saved to the Doctors tab, so there is no need to switch tabs.

3. Open or close a slot

Striped grey cells are closed. Most sites open and close on a sensible default schedule, but you can change any slot for the week you are on: click a closed cell and press Open this slot, or click an open cell and press Close this slot. Closing a slot also clears any doctor in it. These changes only affect the current week.

4. Watch the warnings

Red and amber cells are flagged automatically. The list under the grid explains every one. The tool checks double-bookings, travel time between areas, OHD-only sites, locum-only sites, and the 45-hour limit.

5. Special bookings

If a doctor has an outside commitment (not a roster site), add it on the Special Bookings tab. The tool will then treat that doctor as busy at that time and warn you of any clash.

6. Export

Press Export PDF or Export PNG at the top to save the finished roster as a compact, phone-friendly image — no need to screenshot.

7. Add, edit or remove a clinic

On the Clinics tab you can add a new clinic or site: give it a name, an area, how many shift slots it has, and the timing of each slot. Every clinic — built-in or one you added — is listed below with an Edit and a Remove button. Editing lets you rename the clinic, change its area or pool, and adjust, add or remove shift times; the roster keeps up automatically. Removing a clinic clears its rows from every week.

8. Doctor leave

Once you have reviewed and approved a leave application, record it on the Leave tab — pick the permanent doctor and the first and last day off. The roster then marks that doctor in red if they are placed on a day they are away, and the assign popup shows them as unavailable. Leave for the current week is listed above the grid.

9. Public holidays

On the Holidays tab, set a public holiday given by the state or a company: choose the date, give it a name, and tick which clinics are closed (the area buttons tick a whole region at once). Those clinics then show PH in the roster for that day. If a company still needs cover on the holiday, click the PH cell and assign a doctor as usual.

10. Backup

Your work saves automatically in this browser. Use Backup → Download backup file every so often, or to move your data to another computer.
